Delete your AstroNemi account

Last updated: 29 October 2026

You can request deletion of your AstroNemi account and associated personal data from inside the app. You can also request deletion by email if you cannot access the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Open AstroNemi and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

The request enters a 30-day grace period. You can cancel the request during that period from Settings or by contacting support. After the grace period, we hard-delete the account and associated data, subject to the retention exceptions below.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email support@astronemi.app from the email address or phone number associated with your account, with the subject “Account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing. There is no fee.

Before you delete: unused AstroNemi Credits are forfeited when the account is deleted, except where a refund is required by law or store policy. Contact support before deleting if you believe you are entitled to a refund. Non-renewing passes simply end; auto-renewing store subscriptions (if any) must be cancelled separately in your Apple or Google account settings.

What we delete

  • Profile details: name, phone number, email, avatar, language, and preferences.
  • Birth details and location used for Kundli calculations.
  • Saved Kundlis, reports, and personalized content.
  • Consultation messages, consultation history, and reviews associated with your account.
  • Wallet/credit balance and wallet transaction history, subject to records we must retain.
  • Push notification tokens and device bindings.

What we may retain

We may retain limited records where required or permitted by law, including payment, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, chargeback, security, and dispute records. We may also retain aggregated or de-identified information that no longer identifies you. Backup copies are purged on a rolling basis.

Store subscriptions and purchases

Apple and Google manage their own purchase records. Deleting your AstroNemi account does not automatically cancel a store subscription or issue a store refund. Use your Apple or Google account settings to manage purchases, and contact Apple or Google for store refunds.

Processing time

Deletion requests are processed after the 30-day grace period. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days after the grace period, unless a legal or security hold applies. You will lose access to the account once deletion is processed.
