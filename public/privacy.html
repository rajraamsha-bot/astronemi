Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Introduction

AstroNemi (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides astrology, horoscope, Kundli, Panchang, and consultation services through the AstroNemi mobile application and related websites (the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices you have.

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Name, phone number, email address, profile picture, and language preferences.
  • Birth date, time, and place, and optional birth location coordinates, used to generate Kundli and astrology results.
  • Questions, messages, reviews, and other content you submit to astrologers or through the app.
  • Support communications and information you provide when you contact us.
  • Payment-related records such as product, amount, currency, store transaction identifiers, and status. Apple, Google, and Razorpay process your payment instrument; we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system, app version, language, and unique app/device identifiers.
  • Log data such as access times, screens viewed, crashes, and performance diagnostics.
  • Approximate or precise location, only when you grant permission, to calculate planetary positions for your current place.
  • Usage data such as features used, consultations started, and interactions with astrologers.
  • Push notification tokens so we can send the notifications you enable.

3. How we use information

  • Generate personalized astrological calculations, horoscopes, Kundli, Panchang, and reports.
  • Connect you with astrologers for chat, voice, and video consultations.
  • Process AstroNemi Credits, subscription passes, AstroPro passes, and eligible physical or consultation payments.
  • Maintain your wallet/credit ledger and transaction history.
  • Send notifications, reminders, service updates, and offers (you can opt out of marketing).
  • Provide support, investigate reports, prevent fraud, enforce our Terms, and protect users and the platform.
  • Improve the Services, troubleshoot problems, and develop new features.
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

4. Payments and app-store purchases

Digital goods such as AstroNemi Credits, customer subscription passes, and AstroPro passes are sold as in-app purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes those payments and provides us with transaction information needed to verify and fulfill your purchase. Their privacy policies also apply.

Razorpay may be used for physical shop orders and eligible person-to-person consultations or ritual services. We receive payment status and limited transaction details, not your full card number.

AstroNemi Credits are service credits used for eligible app features. See the Wallet Terms for how credits work, expire, and are refunded.

5. How we share information

  • Astrologers you consult: limited information needed for the consultation, such as your name, birth details, and the questions you submit.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting and database services, payment processors (Apple, Google, Razorpay), video/voice infrastructure (Agora), push notification services (Expo/Firebase), crash reporting (Sentry), and customer support tools.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, lawful request, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and property, or to investigate fraud or abuse.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, row-level security, authentication, and monitoring. Our infrastructure providers may also encrypt data at rest.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping your account and OTP secure.

7. Retention and account deletion

We retain personal information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Services, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Account deletion requests enter a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel the request. After the grace period, we delete or de-identify account data, subject to records we must retain by law (for example, payment, tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute records). Backup copies are purged on a rolling basis.

See Account Deletion for the in-app steps and the categories of data affected.

  • Correct inaccurate profile or birth details.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for location, notifications, or marketing, where applicable.
  • Request data portability in a commonly used format.

To exercise these rights, use the in-app settings or email support@astronemi.app. We may need to verify your identity before acting.
